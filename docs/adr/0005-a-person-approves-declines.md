# 5. A person approves every declined claim

## Context
Return orders can be cancelled; a declined claim sent to a customer is a complaint, a review and sometimes a lawyer.

## Decision
Declines (expired, bought outside our countries) always go to review with the draft. Approvals, compatibility answers,
help articles, spare parts and questions for a missing document go out when the check passes.

## Consequences
About 6% of tickets wait for a person for 1.5 minutes each; agents catch most wrong drafts there (85% in the
simulation). The review queue is first in the console.
