A 12V-2x6 connector carries high current over small pins. A plug that is not fully seated heats up.

1. Switch off and unplug the system. Stop using the cable.
2. Push the plug in until there is no visible gap and the latch clicks.
3. Keep at least 35 mm of straight cable before any bend.
4. Use the native cable of a C5 power supply. Do not use adapters on C4 units.
5. Send photos of both ends to support; we replace damaged cables.
