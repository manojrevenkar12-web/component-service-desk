summary_en: The power supply fan stays off at low load on purpose (semi-passive mode) and starts by itself when the unit gets warm; this is normal.

summary_pl: Wentylator zasilacza celowo nie pracuje przy niskim obciążeniu (tryb półpasywny) i sam się włącza, gdy zasilacz się nagrzeje; to normalne.
---
Ember, Tide and Deep power supplies run their fan only when needed. At idle and in office work the fan often stands still.

1. Load the system with a game or a stress test for ten minutes; the fan starts when the unit warms up.
2. If the unit switches off under load or smells hot while the fan stands still, stop using it and open a warranty claim.
