Coil whine is a high-pitched noise from inductors vibrating at high load, usually in games with very high frame rates or in menus.

1. Find the source: run a game, then pause it with the frame rate uncapped. If the whine follows the graphics card load, the card is the source.
2. Cap the frame rate (V-Sync or a frame limiter). Whine at several hundred frames per second is common and harmless.
3. Plug the power supply into a different wall socket and remove power strips with surge filters.
4. If the power supply whines at idle, or the noise is loud enough to hear from a closed case at one metre, open a warranty claim with the invoice.
