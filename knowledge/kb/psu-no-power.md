When nothing happens on pressing the power button after a power supply swap:

1. Set the rocker switch on the back of the unit to I and check the mains cable.
2. Push the 24-pin ATX connector into the mainboard until the latch clicks. A half-seated 24-pin is the most common cause.
3. Connect the CPU 8-pin (EPS) cable. Do not use a PCIe cable in the CPU socket: they are keyed differently.
4. Check that the case power button header sits on the right mainboard pins.
5. Use only cables from this power supply. Modular cables from other units can damage components.
